# Product

## Register

product

## Users

MyCart is used by the owner of a self-hosted installation. The person using an installation can vary, so notification and scheduling settings must be configurable inside the app instead of being tied to the developer. The primary workflow is tracking products, refreshing selected or all prices, reviewing price history, and diagnosing failed scrapes.

## Product Purpose

MyCart gives a self-hosted user one place to track product prices across Indian e-commerce sites. Success means price updates are easy to control, scheduled jobs are configurable, alerts reach the user’s Gmail account, and scrape failures contain enough product context to resolve them.

## Brand Personality

Calm, practical, and trustworthy. The interface should feel focused and operational without becoming visually dense.

## Anti-references

Avoid crowded enterprise-admin layouts, decorative dashboard widgets, disruptive visual redesigns, and controls that hide destructive effects. New features should preserve the existing calm blue interface.

## Design Principles

- Keep common price-tracking actions close to the products they affect.
- Make bulk actions explicit about their selected scope.
- Preserve useful current data when clearing historical data.
- Show failures with enough product and timing context to act on them.
- Keep secrets out of responses and encrypt stored credentials by default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard access. Use clear labels in addition to icons, retain visible focus states, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
